# What A App — Voice & Tone (VOICE.md)

> How **What A App** sounds, in Bangla and English. For the content agent, SEO agent, support, and any
> AI-generated copy. Grounded in `docs/growth/STRATEGY.md` (offer, positioning, funnel) and the brand
> personality in `BRAND.md`.

---

## 1. Voice in one line

**Warm, practical, seller-to-seller — never corporate, never foreign-translated.**

We sound like **a successful Bangladeshi shop owner showing another how they stopped losing orders** —
not a SaaS company, not a Western CRM, not a translation of one. We code-switch Bangla/English the way
real BD sellers type, we name real things (COD, bKash, Pathao, RedX, ২টা রাত / 2am), and we lead with
relief, not features.

---

## 2. Five voice principles

1. **Seller-to-seller, not company-to-user.**
   We've felt the 2am missed message and the FB page ban. We talk from inside the problem.
   *Test:* would a real BD seller forward this to another seller? If it sounds like a brochure, rewrite.

2. **Relief before features.**
   Lead with the feeling ("you can sleep / nothing gets missed"), then the mechanism. Never open with a
   feature list. The product is the proof, not the pitch.

3. **Plain Bangla, plain English — naturally mixed.**
   Use the words sellers use. Don't translate idioms literally. Don't force pure "shudhho" Bangla where
   sellers would say it in English (e.g. "order", "inbox", "COD", "page" stay as-is). Don't force English
   where Bangla carries the feeling.

4. **Concrete and specific.**
   "৳3,000 in missed COD this month" beats "boost your revenue." A real Pathao booking beats "logistics
   integration." Numbers, names, time-of-day, taka amounts.

5. **Calm confidence, honest scarcity.**
   We're the steady one. No hype, no fake urgency, no fake countdowns. Scarcity is only ever real
   (cohort onboarding bandwidth; a genuinely time-limited launch bonus — per STRATEGY.md).

## 3. Words we use / words we avoid

### Use (EN)
own server · ban-proof · never miss an order · while you sleep · from chat to door · your data is yours ·
auto-reply · COD · order · inbox · page · seller · built for Bangladesh · set up in 30 minutes · works on your phone

### Use (BN)
নিজের সার্ভার · বন্ধ হবে না · অর্ডার মিস হবে না · ঘুমের মধ্যেও বিক্রি · চ্যাট থেকে ডেলিভারি · আপনার ডেটা আপনার ·
অটো-রিপ্লাই · ক্যাশ অন ডেলিভারি · দোকান · বাংলাদেশের জন্য · ৩০ মিনিটে চালু · একাই চালান

### Avoid
- **Corporate/foreign:** "leverage", "synergy", "solution provider", "empower your business journey", "seamless omnichannel", "robust", "cutting-edge", "revolutionize", "elevate".
- **Hype/fake urgency:** "🔥🔥 LIMITED TIME", fake countdowns, "act now or lose forever", "GUARANTEED 10X".
- **Over-formal Bangla:** stiff "শুদ্ধ" textbook Bangla a seller wouldn't speak ("আপনার ব্যবসায়িক উন্নতির লক্ষ্যে...").
- **Channel-clone language:** never imply we *are* WhatsApp/Meta or are endorsed by them.
- **Refund-led promises:** BD trust dynamics distrust refunds — we lead with the *service* guarantee ("we sit with you until it works"), not "money back".

## 5. Tone by surface

| Surface | Tone | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Landing hero (World B) | confident, cinematic, short | one promise, big type; relief + ban-proof |
| Problem/feature sections | concrete, calm | numbers + named BD tools (Pathao, bKash) |
| In-app microcopy (World A) | helpful, brief, encouraging | "Done · ৳1,499 saved", never scold the user |
| Social — Survival Guide | empathetic, useful | teach first, no pitch |
| Social — Behind the Store | human, real | seller's own words, light touch |
| Support / WhatsApp replies | warm, fast, plain | answer the worry first, steps second |
| Guarantee / scarcity | steady, honest | service guarantee; real scarcity only |

## 6. How AI-generated content MUST sound

Any agent generating copy follows these hard rules:

1. **Pass the seller-forward test** (§2.1). If it reads like a brochure, regenerate.
2. **Bangla is written, not translated.** Generate Bangla *natively* — do not write English then
   machine-translate. Allow natural code-switching (keep "order", "inbox", "COD", "page", "delivery"
   in English where sellers do). If unsure a Bangla phrase sounds natural, prefer the simpler spoken
   form over the formal one.
3. **One concrete detail per post minimum** — a taka amount, a time (২টা রাত), a named tool, or a real
   scenario. No abstract benefit-only copy.
4. **No banned words** (§3). No emoji-soup, no fake urgency, no foreign-corporate vocabulary.
5. **Relief/feeling first, mechanism second** in any persuasive copy.
6. **Honest scarcity only.** Never invent a countdown or a "only 3 spots" that isn't real.
7. **Respect naming** (`BRAND.md` §0): "What A App" (three words), "The Ecomex Seller Stack" /
   "দোকান চালু সিস্টেম", wedge "Ban-proof · Bangla-first · Chat to delivery". Never expose internal
   provider names.
8. **Match the surface's world & font** when producing creative (World A orange app vs World B violet
   landing — see `BRAND.md` §2 and `SOCIAL-TEMPLATES.md`).

### Self-check the agent runs before publishing
- [ ] Would a Dhaka seller actually type this Bangla?
- [ ] Is there one concrete BD detail (taka/time/tool)?
- [ ] Did I lead with relief, not a feature list?
- [ ] Zero banned/corporate words, zero fake urgency?
- [ ] Naming + wedge correct? No internal provider names leaked?
- [ ] Right world/tone for the surface?
