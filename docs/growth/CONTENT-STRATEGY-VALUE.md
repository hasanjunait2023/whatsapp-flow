# Ecomex Automation — Value-Driven Content Strategy (brain of the autonomous engine)

> Founder directive: always Ecomex-branded, NOT product-pushing. Value-first content
> that grows a loyal organic BD SaaS/owner audience → engagement → funnel. Bengali-first.
> Goal + funnel + feature-derived ideas. Drives the @amijunait video engine (all gated).

## 1. North-star goals (brand-new BD pages)
Leading indicator = SAVE rate + SHARE rate (not likes). BD: Facebook = discovery for
sellers, TikTok compounds on volume, IG Reels rewards consistency, YouTube compounds permanently.

**90-day:** FB 2,000–3,500 · IG 800–1,500 · TikTok 1,500–3,000 · YT 300–600 followers;
eng 6–10% (FB); 150–300 funnel leads total. (5–7 posts/wk hits the floor; 3/wk ≈ 60%.)
**6-month:** FB 8k–15k · IG 3k–6k · TikTok 6k–12k · YT 1.5k–3k; 800–1,500 cumulative leads
→ 80–200 trials → 20–60 paying from organic (≈ ৳60k–105k MRR floor, Pro tier).
Watch: save-to-reach on first 10 posts (>3% = working, <1% = fix hooks before scaling volume).

## 2. Content pillars
1. **বিক্রেতার বাঁচার গাইড (Seller Survival)** — 30%. Ban/COD-fraud/courier/payment rescue. Wins the "should I follow" decision. Ecomex authors, product unmentioned.
2. **অর্ডার অপারেশন (Order Operations)** — 25%. Run 50–100 orders/day, team, COD confirm, returns. Workflows map to product categories (inbox/courier/payment) without naming it.
3. **AI দিয়ে ব্যবসা (AI for Business)** — 20%. Demystify AI: ChatGPT for descriptions, Bangla AI customer service concept. Highest share rate now. "Ecomex = the AI business people."
4. **মালিকের মাথা (Owner Mindset)** — 15%. The 2am-order loneliness, hire-vs-automate, first-100-orders mistakes. Emotional loyalty + @amijunait's human arc.
5. **ব্যবসা বাড়ানোর কৌশল (Growth Playbook)** — 7%. FB ads on small budget, phone product photography, pricing, seasonal (Eid/11.11). Widest net, pure goodwill.
6. **What A App Tips (Feature-as-Education)** — 3%. Only for warm audience; feature shown through the problem it solves, never a feature tour. Lands as the answer they were already seeking.

## 3. The organic funnel
Stranger → (useful hook, save/share/follow) → Warm follower (2–4 wks of pillars 2/3/4) →
Lead (soft CTA → lead magnet via **keyword-comment → DM**: COD Rescue Calculator / 50 Bangla
chat scripts / FB Ban Checklist) → Education+Proof (3-msg DM seq over 7 days, seller-to-seller)
→ Trial (12-min demo + Day-0 setup, "alive moment" = first auto-reply) → Paying (onboarding
milestones + day-45 referral → seller stories become content).

**Comment→DM mechanic** (BD-native, 3–8× link-click): one CTA per post —
"comment করো '[keyword]' — DM-এ পাঠাবো" / opinion question / "এটা কি আপনারও হয়?".

## 5. Engagement + organic growth tactics
- **Cadence:** FB 1/day (peak 8:30–9:30, 13–14, 20–22) + weekly Thu-9pm live; IG 5/wk + daily Stories + ≥3 Reels/wk; TikTok 1–2/day (volume = compound; 14/wk threshold); YouTube 2 Shorts/wk + 1 long/mo (SEO asset).
- **Comment hooks:** binary question · number reveal · "শুধু আমার সাথেই হয়?" open-wound.
- **First-30-min reply window** (velocity signal); **pin the best comment**; **seed 5–8 BD seller FB groups** weekly (1 contribution, no pitch); 1 collab/mo (complementary, not competitor).
- **Hashtags:** FB 3–5 Bangla; IG 10–15 (3 big/4 mid/4 small); TikTok 5–7 broad-BD.
- **Repurpose:** 1 shoot → 5 moments (full Reel/TikTok → 15s hook → carousel → FB text → YT Short w/ SEO title). 6 shoots → 30 moments.
- **Loyal-mentality plays:** weekly public **acknowledgment** of best commenters by name (BD social-recognition loop ≈ +2–3 followers/day); **consistency over virality** (optimize return viewers, not one-time spikes).

## 6. Weekly cadence (the repeatable engine)
- **Mon** Survival (FB+IG Reel, SV) — discovery; keyword→DM.
- **Tue** Order Ops (FB+IG carousel) — warm deepening; opinion Q.
- **Wed** AI (TikTok+Shorts+Reel, SV/RL) — discovery; follow CTA.
- **Thu** Owner Mindset (FB+IG Story, SV personal) — earns the DM; open Q. + Thu-9pm FB Live.
- **Fri** Growth Playbook (FB+IG carousel/text) — widest net; save CTA.
- **Sat** What A App Tips (Reel/TikTok/FB, RL/SV) — warm→trial; one-in-four full product, else 2nd Ops/AI.
- **Sun** Community + repurpose (FB Live recap, IG Story polls) — name top commenter.
**Monthly arc:** W1 theme intro · W2 deep-dive · W3 seller story/proof · W4 lead-magnet push.
